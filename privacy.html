Privacy Policy for IRCTC Rail Tracker

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IRCTC Rail Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how our mobile application and web services function.

1. Information We Collect

IRCTC Rail Tracker is designed to provide Indian Railways train tracking, PNR lookup, and station schedule services without requiring account creation.

  • PNR Numbers and Search Queries: When you enter a 10-digit PNR number or search for train schedules, this data is transmitted securely to railway status APIs to fetch real-time booking and journey records.
  • No Personal Profile Data: We do not collect names, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not track background GPS location.

2. How We Use Information

  • To fetch live PNR booking status, coach and berth allocations, and chart preparation status directly from railway servers.
  • To calculate heuristic confirmation probability estimates for waitlisted tickets.
  • To query train routes, schedules, live running status, and station arrival boards.

3. Data Storage & Retention

We do not store your PNR numbers, passenger manifests, or travel history on our servers. All queries are processed in-memory and discarded once the response is delivered to your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app communicates with verified Indian Railways information providers (RailKit, RapidAPI, Indian Railways NTES feed) solely to retrieve publicly available train tracking data.

5. Security

All network traffic is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). No unencrypted data is transmitted.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for all audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Disclaimer

IRCTC Rail Tracker is an independent utility and is not officially affiliated with IRCTC, CRIS, or the Ministry of Railways. Indian Railways data is provided for informational convenience.
